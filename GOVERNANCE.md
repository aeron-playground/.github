# Governance

How aeron playground is run, who decides, and how that can change.

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Contributor | Anyone who opens an issue, a discussion or a pull request | Proposes changes and takes part in discussions |
| Maintainer | People with write access to a repository | Review and merge pull requests, triage issues and publish releases |
| Owner | [@aeron-gh](https://github.com/aeron-gh) | Runs the organization, its settings and its security, and has the final say |

Each repository names the people who review its code in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

## How we decide

- Most decisions happen in the open, in issues, discussions and pull requests.
- Maintainers look for agreement. When they can't agree, the owner decides.
- Big technical decisions are written down as decision records in the project's docs.
- Security problems are handled in private until a fix is out.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are invited. We look for people who:

- have made good contributions over a few months
- review other people's pull requests with care
- follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/aeron-playground/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

A maintainer proposes a new maintainer, and the owner agrees. New maintainers start with one
repository and get only the access they need.

## Stepping down

Maintainers can step down at any time. To keep the organization secure, we remove access that
hasn't been used for a long time, after asking first.

## Changing this document

Open a pull request. Changes need the owner's approval.
